二、服务说明

2. Service Description

本应用是一款感知式自动开关屏工具：用姿态与接近传感器判断手机的处境，自动点亮或熄灭屏幕（拿起亮屏、倒扣熄屏、进兜锁屏、手拂过顶部点亮），让你不必去碰电源键。为实现该功能，应用会以后台前台服务的方式常驻运行，并在屏幕熄灭后继续接收传感器事件。

为维持免费运营，应用内展示由第三方（Google AdMob）提供的广告；你也可选择观看激励视频换取一段免广告时间，或通过一次性的应用内购买永久移除广告。

The App is a sensor-based screen switch: it uses orientation and proximity sensors to tell what is happening to your phone and turns the screen on or off accordingly (wake when you pick it up, sleep when you turn it face-down or pocket it, wake when a hand passes over the top) so you never have to reach for the power button. To do this it runs as a persistent foreground service and keeps receiving sensor events after the screen goes off.

To keep the App free, it shows ads served by a third party (Google AdMob). You may also watch a rewarded video for a period of ad-free use, or remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.
