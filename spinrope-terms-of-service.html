二、服务说明

2. Service Description

本应用是一款单机物理解谜游戏：画一条线当轨道，让红球滚到青球那里，两球接触即过关。线不是死板的直线 —— 它有物理属性，会下垂、被压弯、卸力后回弹。每关限定笔数（par），每条线用哪种材质由关卡固定分配，玩家不能自行切换：软绳会下垂、回弹、被球压变形，适合做"兜、垫、弹、荡"；硬笔接近刚体，形状几乎不变，适合精确的轨道与穿越窄缝。

失败有两种：笔数用完、全场静止仍未相遇（卡住）；或球碰到尖刺区（立即失败）。过关按用笔数评星：用比 par 更少的笔完成得 3 星，用满 par 得 2 星。游戏含 45 关、分 9 章递进，配备音效、背景音乐、震动与关卡提示开关。

本应用核心玩法完全离线运行，不需要注册账号、不提供云存档、不含排行榜、社交或用户生成内容。仅广告加载需要联网。

为维持免费运营，应用内展示由第三方（Google AdMob）提供的横幅与插屏广告。横幅只在主菜单底部与游戏画面内底部固定位置出现，不遮挡画线操作区；插屏只在关卡结算阶段出现，且第 6 关起才参与计数、冷启动后 60 秒内不展示、两次展示间隔不少于 100 秒、通关最后一关时不展示。广告内容与投放行为由广告商及其平台负责，受其各自的条款与政策约束。

The App is a casual offline physics puzzle game: draw a line as a track and let the red ball roll to the cyan ball — they meet, you clear the level. Your line is not a rigid plank: it is simulated, so it sags, gets pressed out of shape, and springs back when unloaded. Each level grants a fixed number of pens, and the material of each pen is assigned by the level — you cannot switch it yourself. Soft rope sags, bounces and deforms, good for catching, padding, springing and swinging; the hard pen is near-rigid and holds its shape, good for precise tracks and squeezing through narrow gaps.

There are two ways to fail: run out of pens with everything at rest and the balls still apart (stuck), or let a ball touch the spikes (instant fail). Cleared levels are rated by pens used: fewer than par earns 3 stars, exactly par earns 2 stars. The App includes 45 levels across 9 chapters, plus sound, background music, vibration and hint toggles.

Core gameplay runs fully offline: no account, no cloud saves, no leaderboard, no social features, no user-generated content. Only ad loading needs a network connection.

To stay free, the App shows banner and interstitial ads from a third party (Google AdMob). Banners appear only at the bottom of the main menu and the bottom of the in-game screen, in fixed positions that never cover the drawing area. Interstitials appear only at level results, only from level 6 onward, never within 60 seconds of a cold start, never less than 100 seconds after the previous one, and never when you clear the final level. Ad content and delivery are the responsibility of the advertiser and its platform, governed by their own terms and policies.
