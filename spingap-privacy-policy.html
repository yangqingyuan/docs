一句话总结

The Short Version

SpinGap（包名 com.guanren.spingap，以下简称"本应用"）是一款单机益智游戏。本应用不要求注册账号、不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片或任何可直接识别你身份的信息，也不需要这些权限。你的游玩进度（关卡解锁、星数、皮肤、音效与震动开关）只保存在你手机的应用私有目录里。本应用不运营任何自有服务器，没有云端存档，也没有排行榜。

为让游戏保持免费，应用内会展示第三方广告（Google AdMob）。这些广告 SDK 会按行业惯例收集设备广告标识符等用于投放与计量的信息，详见第五章。

SpinGap ("the App", package com.guanren.spingap) is an offline puzzle game. The App requires no account and collects no name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifying information, and it does not request those permissions. Your progress (level unlocks, stars, ball skin, sound and haptics toggles) is stored only in the App's private storage on your own phone. The App runs no server of its own, has no cloud saves, and has no leaderboard.

To keep the game free, third-party ads (Google AdMob) are shown in the App. Those ad SDKs collect standard information such as the advertising ID for delivery and measurement — see Section 5.

本政策说明本应用在你的设备上读写哪些数据、申请哪些权限，以及你对自己的数据拥有哪些权利。本政策仅适用于 SpinGap 应用本体，不适用于你通过系统浏览器打开的第三方网站（如广告落地页或应用商店页面），那些网站适用它们各自的隐私政策。

This policy explains what data the App reads and writes on your device, which permissions it uses, and what rights you have over your own data. It applies only to the SpinGap app itself, not to third-party websites you open through your browser (such as ad landing pages or store listings), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not collect your personal data and we upload nothing to a developer server (the App has no developer server). Specifically:

五、第三方服务（广告）

5. Third-Party Services (Ads)

本应用集成 Google AdMob（Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited）展示横幅广告与插屏广告，以维持免费运营。广告 SDK 可能收集并使用以下常规信息用于投放、频次控制与效果统计：

  • 设备广告标识符（Advertising ID）；
  • 设备型号、操作系统版本、语言与大致地区（由 IP 推断的国家/城市级，非精确定位）；
  • 网络状态（Wi-Fi / 移动网络）、IP 地址；
  • 广告的展示与点击事件。

这些信息由 Google 依据其隐私政策处理，不经过我们（本应用没有服务器，我们也拿不到这些数据）。Google 隐私政策：policies.google.com/privacy

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) to show banner and interstitial ads and stay free. The ad SDK may collect and use routine information for delivery, frequency capping, and measurement:

  • Advertising ID;
  • Device model, OS version, language, and coarse region inferred from IP (city/country level, not precise location);
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / mobile) and IP address;
  • Ad impression and click events.

This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy and never passes through us (the App has no server, and we cannot access it). Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

广告的展示规则（我们把打扰压到最低）

How ads are shown (kept minimal)

  • 横幅固定在主菜单、选关页与游戏画面内的固定位置，不遮挡操作区；
  • 插屏只在关卡结算阶段出现，且第 6 关起才参与、每通关 3 关最多展示 1 次；
  • 冷启动后 2 分钟内一律不展示插屏；
  • 通关最后一关时不展示插屏，把画面留给你；
  • 游玩过程中不会无预期弹出广告。
  • Banners sit in a fixed position on the main menu, level select, and in-game screen, never covering your controls;
  • Interstitials appear only at level results, only from level 6 onward, and at most once per three level clears;
  • No interstitial at all within two minutes of a cold start;
  • No interstitial when you clear the final level;
  • No unexpected ads mid-play.

七、儿童隐私

7. Children's Privacy

本应用面向 13 周岁及以上的一般用户，不面向儿童，也不会在知情情况下收集 13 岁以下儿童（或所在司法辖区规定的相应年龄以下儿童）的信息。首次启动时会明确提示年龄门槛。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，也不含社交、聊天、用户生成内容或内购，儿童的隐私风险被降到最低。若你作为监护人认为孩子正在使用本应用并希望删除其数据，直接卸载应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据即可。

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). The age threshold is shown at first launch. Since the App collects no personal information and has no social features, chat, user-generated content, or in-app purchases, privacy risk for children is minimal. If you are a guardian and want a child's data removed, just uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings.

八、数据安全

8. Data Security

本应用产生的数据存放在 Android 应用私有目录，受系统沙箱保护，其他应用无法读取（在未 root 的设备上）。广告请求使用 HTTPS 传输。由于我们根本没有服务器、也没有你的账号，也就不存在"服务器被拖库导致你数据泄露"这种情形。请自行注意：若设备已解锁或借给他人使用，本机进度是可见的。

App data lives in Android's private app directory, protected by the system sandbox and unreadable by other apps (on non-rooted devices). Ad requests use HTTPS. Because we run no server and hold no account of yours, there is no scenario in which a server breach exposes your data. Please note: if your device is unlocked or shared, the local progress is visible to whoever uses it.
