二、服务说明

2. Service Description

本应用是一款单机休闲益智游戏：点击屏幕把针射入旋转的球体，避开已插入的针与禁插区，插满即过关。任何一次失误（撞到已插入的针，或砸在禁插区上）都会立即导致本关失败；过关后按平均出针间隔评定 1–3 星，出针越快星级越高。包含关卡模式（分章节递进）、无限模式、球体皮肤与音效 / 震动设置。

本应用核心玩法完全离线运行，不需要注册账号、不提供云存档、不含排行榜、社交或用户生成内容。仅广告加载需要联网。

The App is a casual offline puzzle game: tap the screen to shoot a needle into a spinning ball while avoiding needles already stuck in it and banned zones — fill it to clear the level. Any single mistake (hitting an existing needle or landing in a banned zone) immediately fails the level. Cleared levels are rated 1–3 stars by average shot interval: the faster you shoot, the more stars. It includes level chapters, an endless mode, ball skins, and sound/haptics settings.

Core gameplay runs fully offline: no account, no cloud saves, no leaderboard, no social features, no user-generated content. Only ad loading needs a network connection.

To stay free, the App shows banner and interstitial ads from a third party (Google AdMob). Banners sit in a fixed position on the main menu, level select, and in-game screen. Interstitials appear only at level results, only from level 6 onward, at most once per three level clears, never within two minutes of a cold start, and never when you clear the final level. Ad content and delivery are the responsibility of the advertiser and its platform, governed by their own terms and policies.
