2. Service Description

本应用是一款本地数字滑块拼图益智游戏（又称"数字华容道"），提供 3×3、4×4、5×5 三档难度，目标是以尽量少的步数与用时将打乱的数字方块按顺序复原，并记录各档难度下的最佳成绩。

The App is a local sliding-puzzle game (also known as a "number Hua Rong Dao" / 15-puzzle style game), offering 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 difficulty levels. The goal is to restore the shuffled numbered tiles into order in as few moves and as little time as possible, with your best result recorded for each difficulty.

Local play is fully offline: no registration or internet connection is needed, and gameplay and data run and are stored locally on your device. Leaderboards are optional — after signing in to Google Play Games, your best result (fastest time and fewest moves) for each difficulty is uploaded to Google's leaderboard servers and shown under your Play nickname; sign-in, accounts, and related data are handled by Google under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the App can never read your password. You may sign out anytime in the "Leaderboards" panel; after signing out the App uploads no new scores.
