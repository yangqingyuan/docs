一句话总结

The Short Version

SpinRope（中文名「一线牵」，包名 com.guanren.spinrope，以下简称"本应用"）是一款单机物理解谜游戏。本应用不要求注册账号、不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片或任何可直接识别你身份的信息，也不需要这些权限。你的游戏进度（已通关到第几关、每关最好成绩）与设置偏好只保存在你手机的应用私有目录里。本应用不运营任何自有服务器，没有云端存档，也没有排行榜。

为让游戏保持免费，应用内会展示第三方广告（Google AdMob）。这些广告 SDK 会按行业惯例收集设备广告标识符等用于投放与计量的信息，详见第五章。

SpinRope ("the App", package com.guanren.spinrope) is an offline physics puzzle game. The App requires no account and collects no name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifying information, and it does not request those permissions. Your progress (how far you have cleared, your best result per level) and your preferences are stored only in the App's private storage on your own phone. The App runs no server of its own, has no cloud saves, and has no leaderboard.

To keep the game free, third-party ads (Google AdMob) are shown in the App. Those ad SDKs collect standard information such as the advertising ID for delivery and measurement — see Section 5.

一、适用范围

1. Scope

本政策说明本应用在你的设备上读写哪些数据、申请哪些权限，以及你对自己的数据拥有哪些权利。本政策仅适用于 SpinRope 应用本体，不适用于你通过系统浏览器打开的第三方网站（如广告落地页或应用商店页面），那些网站适用它们各自的隐私政策。

This policy explains what data the App reads and writes on your device, which permissions it uses, and what rights you have over your own data. It applies only to the SpinRope app itself, not to third-party websites you open through your browser (such as ad landing pages or store listings), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

二、我们收集哪些信息

2. What Information We Collect

我们不收集你的个人数据，也不向开发者服务器上传任何内容（本应用没有开发者服务器）。具体来说：

  • 不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证件、地址等身份信息；
  • 不读取位置、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相册、文件；
  • 不使用麦克风、摄像头；
  • 不内置统计分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK，不做用户画像，不跨应用追踪你；
  • 没有账号体系、没有云同步、没有排行榜、没有社交分享；
  • 唯一的对外网络请求来自广告服务（第五章）。

We do not collect your personal data and we upload nothing to a developer server (the App has no developer server). Specifically:

  • No name, phone number, email, identity document, or address;
  • No access to location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library, or files;
  • No microphone or camera use;
  • No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no profiling, no cross-app tracking;
  • No accounts, no cloud sync, no leaderboard, no social sharing;
  • The only outbound network traffic comes from the ad service (Section 5).

三、本应用会存哪些数据（全部只在本机）

3. What the App Stores (All Local)

这些数据存放在 Android 应用私有目录下两个 SharedPreferences 文件里（spinrope_progress 存进度、spinrope_settings 存偏好与首启表态），其他应用无法读取。卸载应用或"清除应用数据"即全部删除。

This data lives in two SharedPreferences files inside the App's private directory on Android (spinrope_progress for progress, spinrope_settings for preferences and the first-launch choice), unreadable by other apps. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its app data, deletes it permanently — there is no cloud copy anywhere to restore from.

五、第三方服务（广告）

5. Third-Party Services (Ads)

本应用集成 Google AdMob（Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited）展示横幅广告与插屏广告，以维持免费运营。广告 SDK 可能收集并使用以下常规信息用于投放、频次控制与效果统计：

  • 设备广告标识符（Advertising ID）；
  • 设备型号、操作系统版本、语言与大致地区（由 IP 推断的国家/城市级，非精确定位）；
  • 网络状态（Wi-Fi / 移动网络）、IP 地址；
  • 广告的展示与点击事件。

这些信息由 Google 依据其隐私政策处理，不经过我们（本应用没有服务器，我们也拿不到这些数据）。Google 隐私政策：policies.google.com/privacy

若你位于欧洲经济区（EEA）或英国，本应用会在首次启动时通过 Google 官方的用户同意平台（UMP）展示同意表单；在你表态之前，广告 SDK 不会发出广告请求。表单的具体选项由 Google 提供，你也可以随时在系统"设置 → 隐私 → 广告"中重置或删除广告标识符、关闭个性化广告。

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) to show banner and interstitial ads and stay free. The ad SDK may collect and use routine information for delivery, frequency capping, and measurement:

  • Advertising ID;
  • Device model, OS version, language, and coarse region inferred from IP (city/country level, not precise location);
  • Network type (Wi-Fi / mobile) and IP address;
  • Ad impression and click events.

This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy and never passes through us (the App has no server, and we cannot access it). Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, the App shows a consent form at first launch through Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP); no ad request is made before you have responded. The form's options are provided by Google, and you can additionally reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in system Settings → Privacy → Ads at any time.

EEA/UK 之外的用户不会在应用内被询问广告偏好：个性化广告的开关由 Android 系统本身提供（「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」），你可以在那里关闭个性化广告或重置广告标识符。应用首次启动时只请你确认已阅读并接受《用户协议》与《隐私政策》；不接受可以直接退出。

Outside the EEA/UK, the App does not ask you about ad preferences: the switch for personalized ads is provided by Android itself (Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID. At first launch the App only asks you to confirm that you have read and accepted the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; declining exits the App.

广告的展示规则（我们把打扰压到最低）

How ads are shown (kept minimal)

  • 横幅只有两处：主菜单底部、游戏画面内底部，都在固定位置参与正常排版，不遮挡画线区域；
  • 插屏只在关卡结算阶段出现，且第 6 关起才参与计数；
  • 结算页会先让你看清成绩，约 1.2 秒后才可能弹插屏；期间你点了「下一关 / 重玩」，广告就不会出现；
  • 连续通关 2 次、或通关 1 次加失败 2 次，才攒够一次展示额度；
  • 冷启动后 60 秒内、以及两次展示间隔不足 100 秒时，一律不展示；
  • 通关最后一关时不展示插屏，把画面留给你；
  • 失败只按一半权重计入，且不允许连续两次都由失败触发 —— 不在你挫败的时候叠加广告；
  • 游玩过程中不会无预期弹出广告。
  • Only two banner placements exist: the bottom of the main menu and the bottom of the in-game screen. Both sit in the normal layout flow and never cover the drawing area;
  • Interstitials appear only at level results, and only from level 6 onward;
  • The result screen shows your score first — an interstitial can only appear about 1.2 seconds later, and tapping "Next" or "Replay" in that window cancels it;
  • It takes two wins, or one win plus two fails, to accumulate one ad's worth of credit;
  • No interstitial within 60 seconds of a cold start, and never less than 100 seconds after the previous one;
  • No interstitial when you clear the final level;
  • A fail counts only half, and two fails in a row can never trigger an ad — we do not stack ads on top of frustration;
  • No unexpected ads mid-play.

七、儿童隐私

7. Children's Privacy

本应用面向 13 周岁及以上的一般用户，不面向儿童，也不会在知情情况下收集 13 岁以下儿童（或所在司法辖区规定的相应年龄以下儿童）的信息。首次启动时会有准入页明确提示年龄门槛（13 周岁及以上）与隐私摘要，需你确认接受条款后才能进入游戏。应用会向 Google 的同意平台声明"不面向儿童"（setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent(false)），因此不会进入儿童专用同意模式。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，也不含社交、聊天、用户生成内容或内购，儿童的隐私风险被降到最低。若你作为监护人认为孩子正在使用本应用并希望删除其数据，直接卸载应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据即可。

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). A first-launch screen states the 13+ age threshold and a privacy summary, and requires you to confirm acceptance of the terms before you can play. It declares to Google's consent platform that it is not directed at children (setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent(false)), so it never enters a child-directed consent mode. Since the App collects no personal information and has no social features, chat, user-generated content, or in-app purchases, privacy risk for children is minimal. If you are a guardian and want a child's data removed, just uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings.

八、数据安全

8. Data Security

本应用产生的数据存放在 Android 应用私有目录，受系统沙箱保护，其他应用无法读取（在未 root 的设备上）。广告请求使用 HTTPS 传输。由于我们根本没有服务器、也没有你的账号，也就不存在"服务器被拖库导致你数据泄露"这种情形。请自行注意：若设备已解锁或借给他人使用，本机进度是可见的。

App data lives in Android's private app directory, protected by the system sandbox and unreadable by other apps (on non-rooted devices). Ad requests use HTTPS. Because we run no server and hold no account of yours, there is no scenario in which a server breach exposes your data. Please note: if your device is unlocked or shared, the local progress is visible to whoever uses it.
